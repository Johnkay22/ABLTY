How to Scan

Hold your attention on the scanner and move slowly with intention. The prototype measures repeated dwell near the same zone, smooth movement, and low jitter.

  • Each scan runs for 15 seconds unless an early lock is detected.
  • Signal Contacts increase only after meaningful dwell and revisits.
  • No future signal is selected until lock or timer completion.
