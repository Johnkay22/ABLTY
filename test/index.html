02

MAKE YOUR ACCOUNT

You can poke around as a guest, but an account is what unlocks the dream tools, syncs your data, and lets me turn on Premium for you.

  1. Open ABLTY from the home-screen icon.
  2. Go to Settings and sign up with an email and password, or with Google.
  3. If you used email, you'll get a confirmation message. Tap the link in it.
  4. That link opens your browser and says "Email verified — browser mode disabled." That's the expected result. Close the tab and reopen ABLTY from the icon — you'll already be signed in.
If the email doesn't show up
Check spam and promotions. Still nothing after a few minutes, message me and I'll confirm the account manually.
03

GET PREMIUM TURNED ON

As a tester you get full Premium access, free — no card, no checkout, nothing to cancel. I switch it on by hand on my end.

  1. Once your account exists, send me the email address you signed up with.
  2. I'll flip your account to Premium — usually within a few hours.
  3. Fully close ABLTY (swipe it out of your recent apps) and reopen it from the icon. Premium unlocks on the next open.
  4. Check Settings — your tier should read PREMIUM. If it still says Free, force-close and reopen once more, then tell me.
What Premium opens up
Unlimited remote viewing sessions (free is capped at 2 a day), the full WBTB Re-Entry Protocol including the RC drill and lucidity reading, your ability profile, community targets and leaderboards, and session export.
