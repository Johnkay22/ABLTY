HOW IT WORKS

Every arena is the same experiment wearing a different skin. A batch of random bits is generated and sealed before you begin. You set an intention, then the bits play out in front of you. The question is whether the stream leans your way more than chance allows.

Protocol

  • HIGH — intend the stream toward the first outcome (right, up, image A).
  • LOW — intend it toward the second outcome.
  • BASELINE — watch without intending. This is the control.
  • Runs are fixed length. You cannot stop early, so the statistics stay honest.
  • The seal hash is shown before the run and confirmed after, so nothing is chosen in hindsight.

Arenas

  • Cascade — modeled on the PEAR lab's Random Mechanical Cascade: 9,000 balls through 330 pegs into 19 bins. Here, each peg bounce is one random bit. Push the pile.
  • Drift — the classic REG random walk with PEAR's cumulative-deviation plot and parabolic envelope.
  • Emerge — modeled on PEAR's ArtREG: two superimposed images, the random stream decides which one surfaces.

Lineage
